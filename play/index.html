How to Play

        

          

            
Touch

            
    
              
  • D-pad — walk (slide between arrows)
    • 
              
  • ◆ — jump · double-tap to fly
    • 
              
  • Drag — look around
    • 
              
  • Tap a block — place
    • 
              
  • Hold a block — break
    • 
              
  • Double-tap ▲ — sprint
    • 
              
  • ••• — all blocks
    • 
            

          

          

            
Keyboard + mouse

            
    
              
  • WASD — walk · Ctrl sprint
    • 
              
  • Space — jump · double-tap to fly
    • 
              
  • Shift — fly down
    • 
              
  • Left click — break
    • 
              
  • Right click — place
    • 
              
  • Middle click — pick block
    • 
              
  • 1-9 / wheel — hotbar · E blocks
    • 
            

          

        

        
Tip: tap TNT to light it. Stand back!
