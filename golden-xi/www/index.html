How to play

  • ◉ Left stick — move your active player (nearest to the ball auto-switches to you).
  • PASS — ground pass toward your run. Hold for more power. On defense: Switch player.
  • THROUGH — threaded ball into space ahead. On defense: Tackle.
  • SHOOT — hold to charge power, release inside the green window for a timed finish bonus. On defense: Slide.
  • SPRINT — hold to sprint (uses stamina). On defense: Contain.

Desktop: WASD/Arrows move · J pass · K through · L shoot (hold) · Shift sprint.
